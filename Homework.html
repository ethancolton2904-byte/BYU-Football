Introduction to Baseball

Baseball is a popular sport played between two teams of nine players each. Each of the nine positions are listed below.

  1. Pitcher
  2. Catcher
  3. First Base
  4. Second Base
  5. Third Base
  6. Shortstop
  7. Left Field
  8. Center Field
  9. Right Field

Every position has a unique responsibility in the game that contributes to the teams ability to win. The positions must work in unison to achieve victory. The pitcher throws the ball, the catcher receives it, and the fielders position themselves to catch or field the ball.

The Best teams in baseball are listed below
